# Gluten-Free German Pancake / Dutch Baby

## Status

- Status: CONFIRMED
- Version: 1.0
- Category: breakfast
- Last tested:

## Summary

Gluten-free German pancake / Dutch baby made in a hot 9x13 metal baking pan. Designed for dramatic edge rise, crisp perimeter, and custardy center.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Prep |
|---|---:|---|
| Eggs | 6 large | Room temp |
| Milk | 360 g | 105-115 F |
| Rice flour | 70 g | - |
| Potato starch | 25 g | - |
| Sugar | 30 g | - |
| Salt | 3 g | Fine |
| Vanilla | 5 g | - |
| Butter | 60 g | For pan |

Optional:

- 150-200 g blueberries
- 150-200 g cherries

## Equipment

- 9x13 metal baking pan preferred
- Large whisk
- Mixing bowl

## Oven setup

- Oven temperature: 425 F
- Rack: Center rack
- Heat empty pan for 10 minutes minimum

## Method

### Stage 1 — Eggs

Use aggressive whisking.

1. Whisk eggs, sugar, and salt for 45-60 seconds.
2. Mixture should lighten slightly.

Texture checkpoint:

- Slight foam
- Uniform color

### Stage 2 — Milk

1. Slowly drizzle warm milk while whisking continuously.

### Stage 3 — Dry ingredients

1. Add rice flour and potato starch.
2. Whisk until smooth.

Texture checkpoint:

- Thin batter
- Similar to crepe batter
- No starch pockets

### Stage 4 — Vanilla

1. Add vanilla and whisk briefly.

## Rest

- Rest batter for 10-15 minutes.
- Whisk again briefly before baking.

## Pan loading

1. Add butter to preheated pan.
2. Return to oven for 60-90 seconds until aggressively foaming.
3. Pour batter immediately.
4. Scatter fruit on top if using.
5. Do not stir.

## Bake

Bake at 425 F for 18-22 minutes.

Correct finish:

- Dramatic rise on edges
- Deep golden perimeter
- Slight center wobble
- Crisp edges
- Custardy center

Avoid:

- Opening oven early
- Underheated pan
- Overmixing after rest

- Butter foaming in pan: 60-90 seconds

## Texture targets

- Thin batter similar to crepe batter with no starch pockets.
- Dramatic rise on edges.
- Deep golden perimeter.
- Slight center wobble.
- Crisp edges.
- Custardy center.

## Gluten-free / celiac notes

- Confirm rice flour, potato starch, vanilla, and any fruit additions are gluten-free and safe for celiac needs.
- Use a clean pan and clean utensils to avoid cross-contact.
- If using fruit, verify it has not been processed with gluten-containing ingredients or cross-contact risk.

## Low-carb notes

- Not strictly low-carb because it uses rice flour, potato starch, sugar, and milk.
- Fruit is optional and will increase carbohydrates.
