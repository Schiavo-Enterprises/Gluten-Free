## Gluten-free / celiac notes

- Confirm all sourdough discards are from gluten-free starters only.
- If using oat discard, use only certified gluten-free oats and oat flour because oats are high risk for gluten cross-contact.
- Confirm psyllium and yeast are gluten-free and safe for celiac needs.
- Avoid using shared baking stones, steels, pans, or surfaces that have been used for wheat pita or bread unless thoroughly cleaned and safe for celiac use.

## Low-carb notes

- Not low-carb. Quinoa, sorghum, and oat discard are grain-based or starch-containing components.
