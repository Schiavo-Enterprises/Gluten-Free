## Summary

Reference recipe for Greek lentil soup, also called fakes. This is a traditional/background ratio and method, not yet a confirmed house recipe.

## Base ratio

- 300 g lentils
- 1.2-1.5 L liquid

## Method

1. Saute onion and carrot in olive oil for 6-8 minutes.
2. Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
3. Add lentils, liquid, bay leaf, and oregano.
4. Simmer for 35-45 minutes.
5. Salt after lentils soften.
6. Finish with olive oil and red wine vinegar.

## Texture guidance

- Use lower liquid for thicker stew-style fakes.
- Use higher liquid for soup-style fakes.

## Finish notes

- Vinegar should be added at the end only.
- Olive oil finish is important for body and aroma.

## Texture targets

- Lentils should be tender before salting.
- Final texture can range from stew-style to soup-style depending on liquid.
- Finished soup should have body from olive oil and brightness from red wine vinegar.

## Gluten-free / celiac notes

- Lentils are naturally gluten-free, but dry lentils should be sorted and rinsed carefully because agricultural cross-contact can occur.
- Confirm broth or liquid, spices, vinegar, and any tomato products are gluten-free and safe for celiac needs.
- Use clean cookware and utensils to avoid cross-contact.

## Low-carb notes

- Not low-carb because lentils are carbohydrate-containing legumes.
- Suitable as a gluten-free savory reference recipe, not a low-carb staple.
