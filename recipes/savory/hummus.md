## Gluten-free / celiac notes

- Hummus is usually naturally gluten-free, but confirm tahini, canned or cooked chickpeas, spices, and any packaged ingredients are labeled gluten-free or verified safe for celiac needs.
- Watch for spice cross-contact, especially cumin or blends.
- Serve with gluten-free dippers only.

## Low-carb notes

- Not low-carb because chickpeas are legume-based and carbohydrate-containing.
- Use as a gluten-free savory dip rather than a low-carb staple.
