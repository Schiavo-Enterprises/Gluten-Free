# Gluten-Free Almond Cookies (杏仁饼)

## Status

- Status: CONFIRMED
- Version: 1.0
- Category: desserts
- Last tested:

## Summary

Gluten-free almond cookies made with almond flour, ground roasted almonds, rice flour, potato starch, butter, sugar, egg, and vanilla. Designed for pale matte tops, lightly golden bottoms, and minimal cracking.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Almond flour | 150 g |
| Roasted almonds | 50 g |
| Rice flour | 160 g |
| Potato starch | 80 g |
| Baking powder | 6 g |
| Salt | 2 g |
| Sugar | 120 g |
| Unsalted butter | 120 g |
| Egg | 1 large |
| Vanilla | 5 g |

Topping:

- Whole almonds for topping

## Equipment

- KitchenAid stand mixer
- Paddle attachment
- Sheet pans
- Parchment

## Oven setup

- Oven temperature: 325 F
- Pan: Sheet pans lined with parchment

## Dry prep

1. Grind roasted almonds with about 20 g sugar.
2. Texture should remain sandy with tiny bits.

## Dry mix

Whisk together:

- Almond flour
- Ground almonds
- Rice flour
- Potato starch
- Baking powder
- Salt

## Method

### Stage 1 — Butter/sugar creaming

- Attachment: Paddle
- Speed 2 for 30 seconds
- Speed 4 for 60-90 seconds

Texture checkpoint:

- Lightened slightly
- Smooth
- Not fluffy

### Stage 2 — Egg

- Speed: 2

1. Add egg.
2. Mix 15-20 seconds.
3. Scrape bowl.

### Stage 3 — Vanilla

1. Mix 5-8 seconds only.

### Stage 4 — Dry additions

- Speed: 1 then 2

1. Add dry mix in two additions.
2. Mix only until crumbly dough forms.

Correct dough:

- Slightly sandy
- Holds when squeezed
- Not sticky

## Rest

Rest for 10-15 minutes at room temperature.

## Shaping

1. Portion 25-30 g each.
2. Roll into balls.
3. Flatten to about 1 cm thick.
4. Press almond on top.

Optional:

- Chill 10-20 minutes for cleaner spread control.

## Bake

Bake at 325 F for 15-17 minutes.

Correct finish:

- Pale matte tops
- Lightly golden bottoms
- Minimal cracking

## Cooling

1. Cool 10 minutes on tray.
2. Transfer to rack.

## Timing

- Dough rest: 10-15 minutes at room temperature
- Optional chill: 10-20 minutes
- Bake time: 15-17 minutes
- Tray cooling: 10 minutes
- Total time:

## Texture targets

- Ground roasted almonds should remain sandy with tiny bits.
- Butter/sugar mixture should be lightened slightly, smooth, and not fluffy.
- Dough should be slightly sandy, hold when squeezed, and not be sticky.
- Finished cookies should have pale matte tops, lightly golden bottoms, and minimal cracking.

## Gluten-free / celiac notes

- Confirm almond flour, roasted almonds, rice flour, potato starch, baking powder, vanilla, and whole almond toppings are gluten-free and safe for celiac needs.
- Nuts and baking powder can be cross-contact risks depending on facility and brand.
- Use clean parchment and clean sheet pans to avoid cross-contact.

## Low-carb notes

- Lower-carb than many rice-heavy cookies due to almond flour, but not strictly low-carb because it contains rice flour, potato starch, and sugar.
