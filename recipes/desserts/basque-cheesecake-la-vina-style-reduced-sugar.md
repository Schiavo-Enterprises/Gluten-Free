# Basque Cheesecake — La Viña Style (Reduced Sugar)

## Status

- Status: CONFIRMED
- Version: 1.0
- Category: desserts
- Last tested:

## Summary

Production-style gluten-free Basque cheesecake in the La Viña style, using cream cheese, eggs, heavy cream, potato starch, and reduced sugar. Designed for a deeply browned top, set outer ring, and custardy/molten center.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Prep |
|---|---:|---|
| Full-fat block cream cheese | 1000 g | 68-72 F ideal |
| Sugar | 200 g | Room temp |
| Eggs | 5 large | Room temp |
| Heavy cream | 500 g | Cool but not cold |
| Potato starch | 10 g | Sifted |
| Fine salt | 2 g | - |
| Vanilla extract | 5 g | Or 6 g vanilla bean paste |

## Equipment

- KitchenAid stand mixer
- Paddle attachment
- 9-inch springform pan
- Two large parchment sheets
- Fine mesh strainer
- Instant-read thermometer

## Oven setup

- Oven temperature: 450 F
- Rack: Upper-middle rack
- Preheat: Fully preheat for at least 30 minutes

## Pan prep

1. Lightly grease springform interior.
2. Crinkle two parchment sheets aggressively.
3. Cross-line pan so parchment rises 2-3 inches above rim.
4. Leave wrinkles intact for traditional structure and insulation.

## Method

### Stage 1 — Cream cheese smoothing

- Attachment: Paddle
- Speed: KitchenAid Speed 2

1. Beat cream cheese 3-4 minutes.
2. Scrape bowl and paddle twice minimum.
3. Continue until absolutely smooth.

Texture checkpoint:

- No lumps whatsoever
- Glossy but dense
- No cold streaking

### Stage 2 — Sugar addition

Speed:

- Speed 2 for 1 minute
- Speed 3 for 1 minute

1. Add sugar gradually.
2. Scrape bowl thoroughly.

Texture checkpoint:

- Slightly loosened
- Smooth satin texture
- No grain pockets

### Stage 3 — Eggs

- Speed: KitchenAid Speed 2

1. Add eggs one at a time.
2. Allow each egg to fully disappear before adding next.
3. Scrape halfway through.

Important: Do not whip excess air into batter.

### Stage 4 — Dry addition

- Speed: 1-2

1. Sift potato starch directly into bowl.
2. Add salt.
3. Mix only until incorporated.

### Stage 5 — Cream

- Speed: 2

1. Slowly stream in heavy cream.
2. Mix only until fully smooth.

Texture checkpoint:

- Thick but pourable
- Completely homogeneous
- No foam layer

### Stage 6 — Vanilla

- Speed: 1

1. Add vanilla.
2. Mix 5-10 seconds only.

## Final prep

1. Strain batter through fine mesh strainer.
2. Pour into lined pan.
3. Tap lightly 2-3 times only.
4. Bake immediately.

## Bake

- Begin checking at 28 minutes.
- Typical finish: 30-36 minutes.

Correct pull indicators:

- Top deeply browned with nearly burnt patches.
- Outer 2-3 inches set.
- Center moves like thick custard.
- Internal center temperature: ideal 170-175 F.
- Avoid upper 180s-190s.

## Cooling

1. Cool uncovered at room temperature for 4-5 hours.
2. Refrigerate overnight minimum.

## Serving

Best after 2-3 hours at room temperature.

## Timing

- Prep time:
- Bake time: 30-36 minutes
- Cooling time: 4-5 hours at room temperature
- Chill time: Overnight minimum
- Serving temper time: 2-3 hours at room temperature
- Total time:

## Texture targets

- Custardy center
- Slightly molten interior
- No graininess
- No souffle-like overbaking
- Deeply browned top with nearly burnt patches
- Set outer 2-3 inches with thick-custard center movement

## Gluten-free / celiac notes

- This recipe is naturally gluten-free when all packaged ingredients are verified gluten-free.
- Confirm potato starch, vanilla extract or vanilla bean paste, cream cheese, heavy cream, and any pan-release grease are gluten-free and safe for celiac needs.
- Use clean parchment and a clean springform pan to avoid cross-contact.

## Low-carb notes

- Reduced sugar compared with many traditional versions, but not strictly low-carb.
- Main carbohydrate sources are sugar and potato starch.
